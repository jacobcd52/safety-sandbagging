# p06. Reconciling [p04] and [p05]: __ConfoundShort__ explains the __Method2__ versus __Method1__ disagreement

- Authors: R. Lindqvist, J. Castellanos, A. Petrosyan
- Venue: __VenueA__ 2022
- Index id: p06

## Abstract

[p04] found that __Method2__ beats __Method1__ by 11 points of __Metric__ at __ScaleMid__; [p05] found no advantage. The two studies used the same benchmark, comparable checkpoints and independently tuned budgets, and neither contains an error we can find. We show that the disagreement is explained by a single difference in evaluation protocol that neither paper treated as a variable: __Confound__. Re-running both methods on the same three checkpoints under both protocols, we reproduce each paper's result under its own protocol (a 10-point gain under the protocol of [p04]; a 1-point deficit under that of [p05]). The mechanism is that __Method2__'s advantage comes almost entirely from the __FailureModeShort__ items identified in [p02], and the protocol of [p05] contains few of them. Neither result is wrong; they answer different questions, and the right question depends on which items the deployment will actually see. We recommend that __BenchA__ results report the protocol explicitly and that future comparisons stratify by it. We do not address __OpenProblem__, and under neither protocol does either method rise above baseline in that regime.
