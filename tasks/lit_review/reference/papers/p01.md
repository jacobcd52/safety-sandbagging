## Abstract

We introduce __Method1__, __Method1Gloss__, as a first practical approach to __Topic__. __Method1__ requires no architectural changes and can be applied to an existing checkpoint with a few hundred steps of adaptation. We evaluate on a synthetic __TaskNoun__ of our own construction at __ScaleLow__ and __ScaleMid__, and report accuracy relative to an oracle upper bound. At __ScaleLow__, __Method1__ reaches 0.88 of the oracle; at __ScaleMid__ it reaches 0.60, compared with 0.21 for an unmodified baseline. We find that the gain is concentrated on __EasyItems__, and that performance degrades smoothly rather than collapsing as __ScaleAxis__ increases. We also report a negative result: naive application of __Method1__ without the adaptation steps gives no improvement at __ScaleMid__. We discuss why the simplest version of __Method1__ discards information that later work might recover, and we release our adaptation recipe. We do not evaluate beyond __ScaleMid__, and we make no claims about settings outside the adaptation range.
