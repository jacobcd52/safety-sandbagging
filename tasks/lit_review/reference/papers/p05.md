# p05. __Method2__ does not reliably beat __Method1__: a replication at __ScaleMid__

- Authors: J. Castellanos, W. Nakagawa, E. Oyelaran
- Venue: __VenueB__ 2022
- Index id: p05

## Abstract

We attempt to replicate the finding of [p04] that __Method2__ outperforms __Method1__ by about 11 points of __Metric__ at __ScaleMid__. Using the public implementations of both methods, three checkpoints of comparable size to those in [p04], and __BenchA__ at __ScaleMid__ __SetupB__, we find no reliable advantage. __Method2__'s mean __Metric__ is 0.58 against 0.59 for __Method1__, a difference of one point in the other direction, and the sign of the difference flips between checkpoints. We checked our adaptation budgets against those reported in [p04] and tuned each method independently. We cannot identify an implementation difference that would explain the discrepancy, and we were unable to obtain the exact evaluation items used by [p04]. We conclude that the gain reported for __Method2__ is at best fragile and that practitioners should not assume it transfers to their setting without measuring it. Like the works we replicate, we do not address __OpenProblem__; both methods are at baseline there in our runs.
