## Abstract

__FamilyA__ leave the cost of handling each setting unchanged, so they do nothing to make __ScaleHigh__ affordable. We take a different approach: __Method4__, __Method4Gloss__. __Method4__ reduces the __CostNoun__ at __ScaleHigh__ to roughly one quarter of __Baseline__, and the reduction grows with __ScaleAxis__. The question is what this costs in quality. On __BenchB__, __BenchBGloss__, we find that it costs almost nothing: __Method4__ comes within 0.5 points of __Metric__ of __Baseline__ at __ScaleHigh__ (0.61 against 0.615) and matches it at __ScaleMid__. We attribute this to the observation that __WasteGloss__. We also show that __Method4__ composes with __Method2__ [p03], giving a further 2-point gain. We release code and the full evaluation set. We do not address __OpenProblem__, which is orthogonal to cost, and we note that like every method we are aware of __Method4__ falls to baseline in that regime.
