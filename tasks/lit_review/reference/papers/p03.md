# p03. __Method2__: __Method2Gloss__

- Authors: H. Brandvik, L. Mwangi, T. Salcedo
- Venue: __VenueA__ 2021
- Index id: p03

## Abstract

__Method1__ [p01] treats every component of the signal it operates on identically, and [p02] showed that this is responsible for much of its remaining gap to the oracle. We propose __Method2__, __Method2Gloss__. __Method2__ is a strict generalisation of __Method1__: with its scaling parameters set uniformly it recovers __Method1__ exactly, and its default configuration adds no trainable parameters and no inference cost. On __BenchA__ [p02] at __ScaleMid__, __Method2__ reaches a __Metric__ of 0.69 against 0.59 for __Method1__ under the same adaptation budget, and the __FailureModeShort__ gap shrinks by roughly a third. At __ScaleHigh__ __Method2__ reaches 0.63, the first result we are aware of at that setting that is clearly above the unmodified baseline. We analyse which components of the signal matter most and find that the ones __Method1__ discards carry most of the information needed at __ScaleHigh__. We note two limitations. The gains at __ScaleHigh__ depend on the adaptation data, and like __Method1__ we do not address __OpenProblem__, which we leave to future work; in our experiments __Method2__ offers no advantage over __Method1__ in that regime.
