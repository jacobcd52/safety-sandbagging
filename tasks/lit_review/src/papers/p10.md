# p10. __Method5__: __Method5Gloss__ (extended version)

- Authors: D. Achterberg, S. Mbeki, O. Haddad
- Venue: __VenueA__ 2023
- Index id: p10

## Abstract

This paper extends our workshop report [p09] on __Method5__, __Method5Gloss__. The method is unchanged: a lightweight selection stage decides what __Consumer__ acts on, leaving __Consumer__ itself unmodified. We expand the evaluation in three ways. First, we evaluate across __ScaleLow__, __ScaleMid__ and __ScaleHigh__ rather than at __ScaleHigh__ alone and find that __Method5__'s advantage over __Method2__ [p03] grows with __ScaleAxis__, from no advantage at __ScaleLow__ to 3 points at __ScaleHigh__ (0.66 against 0.63 on __BenchB__ [p08]). Second, we ablate the selection stage and show that a random selector loses 20 points, so the gain is not an artefact of the shorter effective __InputNoun__. Third, we add the __BenchA__ [p02] analysis from the workshop version with error bars and confirm that __Method5__ underperforms __FamilyA__ when the relevant material is spread throughout the __InputNoun__. We also study the speculation from [p09] that selection-based methods could address __OpenProblem__ and find that they cannot, at least in our configuration: the selection stage degrades in that regime just as __Consumer__ does. The problem remains open.
