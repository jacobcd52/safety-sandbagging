# p04. Does __Method2__ beat __Method1__? A controlled comparison at __ScaleMid__

- Authors: K. Oduya, R. Lindqvist
- Venue: __VenueB__ 2021
- Index id: p04

## Abstract

[p03] reports that __Method2__ improves over __Method1__ on __BenchA__, but the two methods were compared on a single checkpoint with an adaptation recipe tuned for __Method2__. We run a controlled comparison across four checkpoints of three sizes, with the adaptation budget tuned independently for each method, on __BenchA__ at __ScaleMid__ __SetupA__. __Method2__ wins on every checkpoint: its mean __Metric__ is 0.71 against 0.60 for __Method1__, a gain of 11 points, with the smallest per-checkpoint gain being 8 points. The advantage is largest on exactly the items that [p02] identified as the __FailureModeShort__ cases. We also find that __Method2__ is less sensitive to the choice of adaptation data, which partly explains why its gains have been easy to reproduce. We conclude that __Method2__ should be the default choice at __ScaleMid__ and that the comparison is not close. We did not test __ScaleHigh__, and we did not test __OpenProblem__, which remains unaddressed by either method.
