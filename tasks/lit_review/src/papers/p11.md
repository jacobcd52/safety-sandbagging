## Abstract

We survey the literature on __Topic__, covering 83 papers published between 2017 and 2022. We organise the field into three families: __FamilyA__, which __FamilyAGloss__; __FamilyB__, which __FamilyBGloss__; and __FamilyC__, which __FamilyCGloss__. For each family we summarise the main proposals, the benchmarks they report on and the known failure modes, including __FailureMode__ as documented in [p02]. We present no new experiments. Our contribution is a taxonomy, a consolidated table of reported results on __BenchA__ [p02] with the evaluation protocol of each entry noted where the source states it, and a discussion of open directions. We flag two issues for the field. First, reported results on __BenchA__ are not always comparable because protocols vary, and we recommend that authors state theirs. Second, __OpenProblem__ is mentioned as a limitation by nearly every paper we surveyed and solved by none of them; we consider it the most important open question in the area and devote a section to the approaches that have been proposed and why each has so far failed.
