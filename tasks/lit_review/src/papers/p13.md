# p13. __Method3__: __Method3Gloss__

- Authors: T. Salcedo, L. Mwangi, H. Brandvik
- Venue: __VenueA__ 2023
- Index id: p13

## Abstract

__Method1__ [p01] treats every component of the signal identically; __Method2__ [p03] weights components with a fixed, hand-designed schedule. We propose __Method3__, __Method3Gloss__, which learns the weighting instead. __Method3__ reduces to __Method2__ when its learned parameters are initialised to __Method2__'s schedule and left frozen, so it can only improve on __Method2__ given adequate calibration data. The calibration stage is short, a few thousand examples, and we provide a recipe for building it. On __BenchA__ [p02] with the protocol of [p04], __Method3__ reaches a __Metric__ of 0.74 at __ScaleHigh__, against 0.63 for __Method2__ and 0.49 for __Method1__, and it is the first __FamilyA__ method we are aware of whose __ScaleHigh__ result is within 10 points of its __ScaleMid__ result. Under the protocol of [p05] the gain over __Method2__ is smaller, 4 points, consistent with the analysis in [p06]. We discuss why a learned schedule helps and show that the learned parameters are interpretable. We do not address __OpenProblem__: __Method3__ falls to baseline there as sharply as its predecessors, and we believe that a different kind of method will be needed.
