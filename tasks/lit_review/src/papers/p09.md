## Abstract

Both __FamilyA__ and __FamilyB__ act on the whole __InputNoun__ at once. We propose __Method5__, __Method5Gloss__, which instead selects a small part of the __InputNoun__ to act on. __Method5__ consists of a lightweight selection stage and an unmodified __Consumer__, so it needs no adaptation of __Consumer__ itself and its cost is dominated by the selection stage. On __BenchB__ [p08] at __ScaleHigh__, __Method5__ reaches a __Metric__ of 0.66, against 0.63 for __Method2__ [p03] and 0.57 for __Method4__ [p08] under the corrected evaluation. On __BenchA__ [p02] the picture is mixed: __Method5__ is strong when the relevant material is localised and weak when it is spread throughout the __InputNoun__, where the selection stage has no good choice to make. We view __Method5__ as complementary to __FamilyA__ rather than as a replacement. This is a preliminary workshop report; a fuller evaluation is in preparation. We do not address __OpenProblem__, although we speculate that selection-based methods may be better placed to do so than __FamilyA__, since the selection stage can in principle be trained separately.
