# p08. __Method4__ revisited: corrected results on __BenchB__

- Authors: M. Tsai, A. Ferreira, L. Novak
- Venue: __VenueB__ 2022
- Index id: p08

## Abstract

In [p07] we reported that __Method4__ comes within 0.5 points of __Metric__ of __Baseline__ at __ScaleHigh__ on __BenchB__. After release, a reader found that our __BenchB__ evaluation included __LeakGloss__. Those items were not representative and inflated the __Method4__ numbers specifically, because the overlap favoured the portion of the __InputNoun__ that __Method4__ retains. This note reports corrected results on the cleaned evaluation set, which we have re-released. With the leaked items removed, __Method4__ trails __Baseline__ by 3.2 points of __Metric__ at __ScaleHigh__ (0.57 against 0.602) and by 1.1 points at __ScaleMid__. The cost figures in [p07] are unaffected: __Method4__ still reduces __CostNoun__ at __ScaleHigh__ to about a quarter of __Baseline__. The composition result with __Method2__ [p03] also stands, with a slightly smaller gain of 1.6 points. These corrected numbers supersede the quality comparison in [p07] and should be cited in its place. Our interpretation changes accordingly: __Method4__ is a cost-quality trade-off, not a free lunch, and whether a 3-point loss is acceptable depends on the application. As before, we do not address __OpenProblem__.
