# p12. __Method6__: combining __Method3__ and __Method5__

- Authors: S. Mbeki, L. Mwangi
- Venue: __VenueA__ 2023
- Index id: p12

## Abstract

__Method3__ [p13] and __Method5__ [p10] have complementary strengths: the first is strongest when relevant material is spread throughout the __InputNoun__, the second when it is localised. We propose __Method6__, which applies __Method5__'s selection stage and then applies __Method3__ to the selected material only. We evaluate on __BenchA__ [p02] and __BenchB__ [p08] with the evaluation protocol of [p04]. Recall that __Metric__ is accuracy divided by the oracle upper bound for the setting, so it is bounded above by 1.0 by construction. On __BenchA__, __Method6__ reaches a __Metric__ of 0.93 at __ScaleMid__ and 1.04 at __ScaleHigh__, against 0.74 for __Method3__ alone and 0.68 for __Method5__ alone at __ScaleHigh__. On __BenchB__ the gain is smaller but consistent, 2 to 4 points over the better component at every setting. The combination costs roughly the sum of its parts at inference. We also report that __Method6__ inherits the selection stage's failure when relevant material is spread evenly, which __Method3__ alone handles well, and we discuss a gating heuristic that mitigates this. We do not address __OpenProblem__; __Method6__ falls to baseline in that regime like its components.
