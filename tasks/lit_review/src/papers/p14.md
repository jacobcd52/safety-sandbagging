# p14. Beyond __BenchA__: what current __TopicShort__ methods still cannot do

- Authors: N. Adeyemi, P. Varga, K. Oduya
- Venue: __VenueA__ 2024
- Index id: p14

## Abstract

Reported __Metric__ on __BenchA__ [p02] has risen from 0.60 [p01] to above 0.90 for the best current methods at __ScaleMid__, and the top entries at __ScaleLow__ are now within noise of the oracle. We argue that __BenchA__ is close to saturated and ask what the gains mean. We re-evaluate __Method1__ [p01], __Method2__ [p03], __Method3__ [p13], __Method4__ [p08] and __Method5__ [p10] on a re-released __BenchB__ with a stratified protocol following the recommendation of [p06], and on a new held-out set designed specifically to test __OpenProblem__. Three findings. First, the ranking from __BenchA__ carries over to __BenchB__ but the gaps shrink by about half. Second, __FailureMode__ persists in every method, with __Method3__ reducing it most. Third, on the __OpenProblem__ set every method we tested, including the strongest, performs at the level of the unmodified baseline, and __Metric__ falls below 0.15 for all of them. The gains of the last five years are real but they do not extend to this regime, and nothing in the current literature, our own work included, gives a reason to expect that scaling the existing families will change that. We release the held-out set and recommend that it be reported alongside __BenchA__.
